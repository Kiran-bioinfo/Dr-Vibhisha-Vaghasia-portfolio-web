Scholarly Profile

Bridging Computational Biology and Experimental Discovery

Dedicated bioinformatics researcher and academic leader with over a decade of research across pathogen genomics, proteomics, vaccine design, and modern artificial intelligence.

"The future of therapeutics lies at the convergence of multi-omics data integration, machine learning architectures, and quantum algorithms that simulate biological complexity at an unprecedented scale."

I hold a Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Gujarat University (conferred January 2026, research period 2018–2024), where my doctoral dissertation provided pioneering proteomic and immunoinformatic insights into Leptospira for unravelling the molecular pathogenesis of leptospirosis. My academic trajectory has traversed rigorous government research fellowships at the Gujarat Biotechnology Research Centre (GBRC) and Gujarat State Biotechnology Mission (GSBTM) under the Department of Science & Technology, Government of Gujarat.

Complementing extensive dry-lab mastery in NGS data analysis, multi-omics integration, structural modeling, molecular dynamics simulations, and epitope mapping, I possess hands-on wet-lab expertise in bacterial culturing, PCR, gel electrophoresis, DNA isolation, and whole-genome/16S sequencing. Today, as an Assistant Professor at Marwadi University, I supervise cutting-edge research projects, including an explainable multi-omics AI framework for predicting HPV-associated cervical cancer progression, while actively expanding into quantum computing applications for biomarker discovery and drug design.
