PROFESSIONAL PROFILE
Bioinformatics researcher and educator with a Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Gujarat University (conferred January 2026) and experience in computational proteomics and drug discovery, genomics, immunoinformatics, NGS analysis, molecular modelling, docking and dynamics, next-generation vaccine design, and emerging quantum technologies. Research experience spans Leptospira pathogenesis and vaccine design, pathogen genomics and proteomics, epitope mapping, multi-epitope vaccine design and computational screening, complemented by wet-lab experience in bacterial culturing, PCR, gel electrophoresis, DNA isolation, and whole-genome/16S sequencing. Currently extending this expertise into quantum computing and AI/ML applications for biomarker discovery, while leading research mentorship, training programmes and multidisciplinary teams in emerging computational technologies.
